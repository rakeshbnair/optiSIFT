The challenge

Metal powder quality is the hidden variable in additive manufacturing. Oxidation, reuse and inconsistent feedstock drive defects, scrap and cost — and today they are usually caught too late.

The platform

From powder signals to process confidence

OptiSIFT closes the loop from sensing to assurance, so you can qualify powder and reuse it with confidence.

SENSE

Advanced sensing and data acquisition capture the optical and thermal signatures of metal powder in real time.

ANALYZE

Intelligent algorithms turn raw signals into clear, quantitative powder-quality metrics.

PREDICT

Processability and performance prediction flag reuse limits and process risk before you print.

ASSURE

Quality assurance and traceability deliver auditable powder qualification across its lifecycle.
